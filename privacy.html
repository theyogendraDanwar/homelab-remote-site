Privacy Policy — Homelab Remote

Last updated: 2026-08-01

Homelab Remote (“the App”) is a remote control for self-hosted services that you configure on your own network.

Support: yogendra.danwar@gmail.com

Data we collect

The App does not collect personal data on our servers. There is no Homelab Remote account and no first-party analytics in the current version.

Data stored on your device

  • Server addresses, API keys, and optional HTTP credentials you enter are stored in the iOS Keychain / on-device secure storage.
  • App preferences (for example language filters and refresh intervals) may be stored in on-device preferences storage.
  • An optional unlock PIN and biometric preference are stored on the device.

Network

The App communicates only with the hostnames and URLs you enter, plus documentation links you choose to open. Credentials are sent only to those hosts.

Optional tips outside India use Apple In-App Purchase (StoreKit). Apple processes those payments; Homelab Remote does not receive your payment card details. In India, Donate may show a Google Pay QR image only.

Third parties

The App is an independent product and is not affiliated with Apple or with any third-party server software you may choose to connect. All trademarks belong to their respective owners.

Contact

Questions about this policy: yogendra.danwar@gmail.com

Deletion

Uninstalling the App removes locally stored credentials and preferences from the device (subject to iOS backup behavior).

The same text is available in the app under Settings → About → Privacy Policy.
